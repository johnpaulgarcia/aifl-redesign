Melbourne, Victoria — spring 2026

Dear reader,

Hello. We’re the Australian Institute of Flexible Learning — a small team of educators in Melbourne who have spent two decades helping people begin, or begin again, in health and community care.

We thought we’d write to you properly, the way we’d speak to you on the phone: no jargon, no pressure, just an honest account of who we are and what we teach. If even one line of this letter sounds like your life, we’d love to hear from you.

* * *

A little about us

We opened our doors in 2005, and we’ve been doing the same thing ever since: empowering people through education, at any age, at any stage. Some of our students are eighteen and just starting out. Some are fifty-eight and starting over. All of them study in a way that fits around work, family and everything else life is doing at the time — because that flexibility is the whole point of us.

Our trainers aren’t lecturers reading from slides. They’re experienced industry practitioners — people who have worked the wards, run the programs and sat in the same community rooms you might one day work in. And wherever you are in Australia, our student support team is a phone call away, from your first enquiry to your final assessment.

We specialise in health, community services, mental health, ageing support, alcohol and other drugs, and business. If you’re in Victoria, some courses carry funded places through the Skills First program; we also deliver VET in Schools programs and subsidised traineeships. Ask us about any of it — it’s a genuine conversation, not a sales script.

Why people stay with us

We think it comes down to three small promises we’ve kept since 2005. You study where you are — anywhere in Australia, around work and life. You’re taught by people who’ve done the work — practitioners, not just presenters. And you’re never a number — when you ring 1300 854 987, a person who knows your course picks up.

Education, empowered, endeavoured. That’s been our quiet motto for two decades — and we mean every word of it.

One small gift, before you go

We’ve written a short e-book on motivation and positive thinking — the same gentle pep-talk we give new students on day one. It’s free, and it’s yours. Reply to this letter at admin@aifl.edu.au with the words “e-book, please” and we’ll send it straight over — no forms, no funnel.

So — if any of this sounds like the nudge you’ve been waiting for, write back. Tell us where you’re at, and we’ll tell you honestly which course fits, what it costs, and whether a funded place might be yours.

Yours, warmly —

the team at AIFL, Melbourne
