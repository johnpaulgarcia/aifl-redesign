03 — The case.

Why people choose AIFL.

Study that bends, not breaks

Flexible delivery built around shift work, caring and everything else life holds. Start at any age, at any stage — the timetable fits you, not the other way round.

Trainers from the floor, not just the front

Our trainers are experienced industry practitioners — people who have done the work in health, aged care, mental health and community services, and now teach it.

Supported, wherever you are

Melbourne-based and Australia-wide. Full student support travels with the course, so studying remotely never means studying alone.

Pathways that can carry the cost

Skills First funded places on selected Victorian courses, VET in Schools programs, and subsidised traineeships. We will walk you through what you are eligible for before you commit.
