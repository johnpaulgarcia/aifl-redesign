Australian Institute of Flexible Learning — ten directions.

Ten complete, self-contained landing-page concepts. Each is a distinct structural and visual system — open any of them in a browser. All concepts drop the regulatory furniture and lead with the school's real story: flexible, supported health and community education, Australia-wide, since 2005.

  1. 14Lotus Chapters — from-scratch story scroll Clean-sheet · The page IS the lotus rising · Interactive petal course filter · gold/teal gradients Five chapters — The Mud, First Light, The Bloom, Tread Lightly, Your Turn to Rise. The course chooser is a hand-drawn SVG lotus: five petals = five categories, click a petal and it blooms gold while filtering the courses. Scroll-progress lotus rail, live search, full story.
  2. 15Nature Editorial — from-scratch premium wellness feel Clean-sheet · Aesop-style editorial · Organic blob/petal image masks · horizontal course gallery Asymmetric full-viewport hero with blob-masked nature photography, snap-scrolling tall course cards with petal-arched tops, pioneer claim as a huge pull-statement, heritage collage with the lotus rising from a mud-to-gold ground bar. Calm, expensive, big-institute.
  3. 13Golden Lotus — the client-briefed final direction Approved finder + tiles · Gold lotus motif · Orange/teal/gold gradients · Nature imagery · pioneer, roots & sustainability story Built to the client's own brief: big-institute scale, the gold lotus risen-from-the-mud as brand emblem, "Australia's online-learning pioneer" band, regional-hospital heritage story, and a "Study online. Tread lightly." sustainability section — on the approved live-search finder and category-tile course chooser.
  4. 12Visual Finder — client-approved direction Image-led · Campus-photo finder hero · Live course search + visual category tiles · Poppins The two pieces the client liked, merged: a live "find the course for you" search hero and five image-led category tiles that filter the grid. Photography carries the page — image-header course cards, photo facts band, image journey steps. Placeholder stock, marked for replacement.
  5. 00The Evolution — their current design, improved Brand-faithful · Orange #f28e37 + teal #009688 · Poppins · clean white institutional Their logo, colours and copy — rebuilt as a bright, white-ground institute site: Poppins throughout, filterable 15-course grid, real facts bar, e-book band.   6. 01The Specimen — tactile editorial Specimen · Atelier · Fraunces + Newsreader · N6 masthead Warm press paper, grain texture, numbered margin sections and type-foundry confidence. The "tactile rebellion" flag-bearer.
  7. 02The Broadsheet — marquee hero Marquee Hero · Newsprint · Newsreader + IBM Plex Sans · N9 edge-aligned The tagline fills the fold like a front page. Column rules, drop caps, and a classified-ad e-book panel below.
  8. 03Night School — dark bento Bento Grid · Bloom · Fraunces + Geist · N5 floating pill Deep green-black darks with one chartreuse glow. Courses as an irregular tiled grid — the "study after hours" mood.
  9. 04The Manifesto — brutalist polemic Manifesto · Brutal · Anton + Geist · N7 slab Poster-sized declaration type on warm paper with a red flood accent. Believes something before it sells anything.
  10. 05Care First — soft optimistic Split Studio · Hum · Plus Jakarta Sans · N12 banner Rounded, warm and friendly. Alternating diptychs, a retracting e-book banner, and a marquee footer — the approachable face.
  11. 06The Ledger — stat-led clarity Stat-Led · Cobalt · Space Grotesk + Geist · N1b SaaS bar Two decades as the hero number. Product-grade discipline: one electric accent, hairlines, and an honest course ledger.
  12. 07The Letter — first-person intimacy Letter · Garden · Fraunces + Newsreader · N1a minimal "Dear reader," — the whole page as a personal note from the institute. Botanical palette, sprig dividers, letter-close footer.
  13. 08The Track — student journey Narrative Workflow · Sport · Big Shoulders + Geist · N10 morph Five stages from enquiry to graduation as a kinetic timeline. Condensed display type, track-lane navigation, scroll-morphing bar.
  14. 09The Aurora — cinematic stack Feature Stack · Aurora · Tomorrow + Geist · N3 side-rail Dark cyan atmosphere with sticky, scroll-synced panels. The most cinematic of the ten — night-sky study sessions.
  15. 10The Print Room — riso catalogue Catalogue · Riso · Bricolage Grotesque + Space Grotesk · N13 ⌘K Every course a printed specimen — misregistered inks, multiply blends, and a keyboard-first course finder.
