Why people choose AIFL

Flexible by design

Work, family, life — study has to fit around all of it. AIFL courses are built to bend: learn at your own pace, at any age, at any stage, from anywhere in Australia.

Trainers who’ve done the work

AIFL trainers are experienced industry practitioners. They’ve worked in aged care, mental health, community services and business — and they teach from that experience, not just from a textbook.

Support that stays with you

From your first enquiry to your final assessment, full student support comes as standard. Funded places are available on some courses through Victoria’s Skills First program, alongside VET in Schools programs and subsidised traineeships.
