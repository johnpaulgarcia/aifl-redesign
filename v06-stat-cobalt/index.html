Ways to fund your course.

Depending on the course and your circumstances, there are real pathways that reduce or remove the cost of study. We’ll walk you through what applies before you commit to anything.

01Skills First — Victoria

Government-funded places on selected courses for eligible Victorian students, including the Certificate IV in Ageing Support.

02VET in Schools

VETiS programs let school students begin a nationally recognised qualification while completing their schooling.

03Subsidised traineeships

Earn while you learn — subsidised traineeship pathways combine paid work with structured training.
