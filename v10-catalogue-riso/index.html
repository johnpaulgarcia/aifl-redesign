Why AIFL

Since 2005

Two decades of empowering people through education. Learn at any age, at any stage — we’ve been doing this long enough to know what support actually looks like.

Flexible by design

Study options that fit around work and life, delivered anywhere in Australia — with full student support from first enquiry to final assessment.

Practitioners as trainers

Your trainers are experienced industry practitioners across health, community services, mental health, ageing support, alcohol and other drugs, and business.

Funding pathways

Skills First funded places (Victoria) on selected courses, VET in Schools programs, and subsidised traineeships. Call us and we’ll walk through what you’re eligible for.
