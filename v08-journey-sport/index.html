Stage 1.0Enquire.

Every enrolment at AIFL starts with a conversation, not a form. Call 1300 854 987 or email the team and talk to a real person about where you are, where you want to be, and which of the 15+ courses gets you there. No pressure, no jargon — just a straight answer on what fits.

Start the conversation

Stage 2.0Pre-training review.

Before you commit, AIFL maps your experience, your goals and your circumstances against the right course — and checks whether funding applies to you. Skills First funded places are available on selected courses for eligible Victorians, alongside subsidised traineeships and VET in Schools programs. You enrol knowing exactly what you’re signing up for.

See the course list

Stage 3.0Flexible study.

Learn at any age, at any stage. Study is built to flex around shifts, kids and everything else life throws at you — available throughout Australia, at your pace, with full student support the whole way. Your trainers aren’t career lecturers: they’re experienced industry practitioners who teach what they’ve done.

Why students choose AIFL

Stage 4.0Work placement.

Qualifications in health and community care are earned with people, not just on screens. Where your course includes placement, AIFL helps you get real hours in real workplaces — aged care, community services, allied health — so you graduate with experience employers recognise, not just a certificate.

Ask about placement

Stage 5.0Graduate.

You cross the line with a qualification in health, community services or business — and the momentum to use it. AIFL has been empowering people through education since 2005: two decades of passionate educators helping Australians retrain, return to work, and step up into care. Your finish line is someone else’s starting gun.
