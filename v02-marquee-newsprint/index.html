Two decades of flexible learning

AIFL has been empowering people through education since 2005. We are passionate educators, and we believe study should fit around work and life — not the other way round. With us, you can learn at any age, at any stage, from anywhere in Australia.

Our trainers are experienced industry practitioners who teach what they have practised. They bring current, real-world knowledge from health, community services, mental health, ageing support, alcohol and other drugs, and business — and they back every student with full support for the length of the course.

Funding should never be the reason someone stops short. Skills First funded places are available on selected Victorian courses, alongside VET in Schools programs and subsidised traineeships. Ask us what you may be eligible for — a single phone call is usually enough to find out.
