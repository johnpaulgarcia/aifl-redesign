Who we are

Educators since 2005

Quality, passionate and innovative educators. AIFL has been established since 2005 — a testament to its commitment to quality education.

We grew out of the need for training that adapts responsively and flexibly to the everyday learning requirements of individuals and allied health businesses.

How it works

From enquiry to graduation

Five steps, supported the whole way. Start with a conversation — call 1300 854 987 — and we check your goals, experience and funding eligibility before you commit to anything.

Then it's self-paced online learning that fits around work, family and life, with placement support so you graduate with real experience, not just theory.
