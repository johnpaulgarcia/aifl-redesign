Why choose AIFL?

Because we are passionate about educating and learning — thriving to empower through education.

Why? Passionate, always

We want you and your community to have you as a compassionate and caring individual, and to love the work you are in. Everyone deserves the opportunity to learn, upskill and excel — with flexible options so you can live your best life while improving yours and others'.

Who? Educators since 2005

Quality, passionate and innovative educators. AIFL has been established since 2005 — a testament to its commitment to quality education. We grew out of the need for training that adapts responsively and flexibly to the everyday learning requirements of individuals and allied health businesses.

What? Supported, at any age

A place where you can learn at whatever age, wherever in life, and feel supported and encouraged. Specialising in Health, Community Services, Mental Health, Ageing Support, Alcohol and Other Drugs and Business — Australia-wide, with experienced industry practitioners.

Questions, answered

Anything else — call 1300 854 987 and ask a person.

Can I really study at my own pace?

Yes — flexible, self-paced learning is the whole reason we exist. Our model lets you juggle work, family and life while still getting ahead in your career. You study when it suits you, with full support from our team the whole way through.

Is government funding available?

Often, yes. Funded places are available through the Skills First program on eligible courses, and we also offer subsidised traineeships and VET in Schools programs. Eligibility depends on your circumstances — we check it with you during your pre-training review, before you commit to anything.

Do you help with work placement?

We do. Courses with a practical component come with placement support — our team works with you to arrange hands-on experience in the sector you're training for, so you graduate with real workplace confidence.

Who will be teaching me?

Experienced industry practitioners — people who have worked extensively in the fields they teach, from aged care and mental health to community services and business. You're learning from people who've done the job, not just read about it.

How do I enrol?

Start with an enquiry — call 1300 854 987 or use the contact form. We'll walk you through a pre-training review to confirm the course fits your goals, sort out funding and your USI, and get you started. No obligation until you're ready.
